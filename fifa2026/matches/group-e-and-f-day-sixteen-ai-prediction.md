They set up to frustrate in a **4-3-3** that folds into a deep 4-1-4-1 block: **Eloy Room** in goal; a back four of **Sherel Floranus**, **Armando Obispo**, **Riechedly Bazoer** and **Deveron Fonville**; the **Bacuna** brothers (Leandro and Juninho) with **Livano Comenencia** in midfield; and **Tahith Chong**, **Jürgen Locadia** and **Sontje Hansen** carrying the counter.
