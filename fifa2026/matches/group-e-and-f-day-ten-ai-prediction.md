### Historical Backdrop
These two have a long, low-key history — roughly 25 meetings all-time stretching back to a 1908 Olympic third-place playoff, with the Netherlands leading the series 12 wins to 8 and 5 draws. The most memorable knockout collision came at **Euro 2004**, a goalless quarter-final the Netherlands edged 5–4 on penalties. Recent competitive meetings have favoured the Dutch — a 2–0 win in October 2017, a 1–1 draw in Sweden the year before. Saturday is only their second-ever World Cup encounter, 52 years after the first.
