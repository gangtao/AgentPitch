That Messi missed and Salah scored, and Argentina still advanced, is the cruellest possible framing for Egypt — the machine says the better side survived, but only just.

### Simulation Report — **Switzerland 3–3 Colombia (a.e.t.) — Switzerland win 4–2 on penalties**
Six goals, three lead changes, and a shootout — the machine's tie of the round. Colombia, the favourites, controlled the ball (**53% possession**) and struck first each time; Switzerland, out-possessed but relentless in front of goal (**23 shots to nine**), refused to die. **Luis Díaz** opened it on **51'**, only for **Ruben Vargas** to level four minutes later on **55'**. **Luis Suárez** nudged Colombia back ahead on **64'**, and again Switzerland answered — **Dan Ndoye** on **85'** to force extra time at 2–2. In the added period **Díaz struck his second on 102'** to put Colombia on the brink of the last eight, but **Vargas completed his brace on 115'**, a gut-punch equaliser that sent it to penalties at **3–3**. Johan Manzambi and Vargas (eight shots apiece) had peppered Camilo Vargas all night; Suárez (five) and Díaz (four) carried Colombia's clinical edge. In the shootout, both captains blinked — **Granit Xhaka and James Rodríguez both missed** — before **Gregor Kobel** saved from **James** and **Luis Díaz**, and Embolo, Freuler, Ndoye and Vargas converted. **Switzerland 4–2**, an upset sealed on nerve.

**AI takeaway:** The machine sees the favourite undone by its own profligacy-in-reverse — Colombia had the ball and the lead three times but could not hold any of them, while Switzerland's sheer volume of shots (23) and Vargas's ice-cold brace kept dragging the tie back. It reads as a coin-flip that broke Swiss: Kobel matched Emi Martínez blow for blow across the two venues, two saves apiece, and where Colombia beat Switzerland at USA '94, the machine hands the Swiss their revenge 32 years on. Lorenzo's side will feel this one for a long time.
