**AI takeaway:** The machine shows the value of patience against a parked bus — and of having the bodies to break it. England's three attacking midfielders carrying the ball into the box, rather than recycling in front of the block, is what turned 57% possession into thirty shots. Tuchel will note the clean sheet and the spread of goals (three different scorers, none of them Kane) as signs his front line can hurt teams that sit. DR Congo's deep block kept it respectable for half an hour, but over ninety minutes the gulf told. England march into the last 16 with a statement.
